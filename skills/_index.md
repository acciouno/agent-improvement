**Status values:** `active` · `retired` (date + reason in Notes). Two states
only — "past review date" and "failed a check" are handled by the review
process, not by extra statuses that need their own bookkeeping.

**Review windows:** 60 days from last validated. The date is a *trigger*,
not a verdict — the retirement decision uses the contribution record
(uses / helped / harmed, aggregated from the experience log's skill-use
entries at review time), not the calendar alone. An idle-but-valuable skill
(e.g. an annual task) stays; an unused-and-unhelpful one goes.

**Active cap:** at most 12 active skills (adjustable by the human; the point
is that it's finite). A new skill must displace an incumbent or justify
growing the cap. Unbounded growth is the library-drift mechanism; the cap is
the brake.
